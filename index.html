05 / HOW I TROUBLESHOOT

Observe. Investigate.
Fix. Verify.

A structured approach to practical system and network problems.

Understand

Reproduce the issue, identify what changed and define the expected behaviour.

Inspect

Check service status, logs, processes, IP configuration, routes and listening ports.

Isolate

Test one layer at a time — host, network, DNS, firewall, service and application.

Resolve & verify

Apply the fix, retest from the client side and document what solved it.
